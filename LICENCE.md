### 🎮 Core Overlay
- Resource Tracker — display platinum, credits, and rare resources in real-time
- Enemy Radar — show enemy positions on minimap
- Damage Meter — track damage dealt by you and your squad
- Mission Timer — countdown and wave timers for endless missions

- Hotkey Manager — assign custom hotkeys for toggles
